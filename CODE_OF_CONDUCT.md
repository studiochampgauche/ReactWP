# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ReactWP is committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community. Participation is open regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, nationality, appearance, religion, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and provide it with useful context.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Prioritize the safety and health of the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discrimination.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, events, and public spaces where someone represents ReactWP or its community.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [security@reactwp.com](mailto:security@reactwp.com). Include relevant links, dates, context, and supporting material. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code.

Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is prohibited. Deliberately false or malicious reports may themselves result in enforcement.
